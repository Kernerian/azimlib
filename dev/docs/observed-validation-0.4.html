Eclipses

Frozen corpus: 82 events (52 solar, 30 lunar). Preserved independent comparisons: 5118/5118 centers within 2 km, maximum observed 1.811 km; 489 solar contacts maximum 8.65248 s; 150 lunar contacts maximum 30.5347 s; 195 magnitudes maximum 0.00063769; 137 solar obscurations maximum 0.000831888.

These maxima describe tested cases and quantities, not bounds between samples, all epochs or unrepresented physical components. 10162 earlier boundary checks contain 9941 numerical matches to the represented graph and 221 unresolved checks; graph distance does not certify the complete physical boundary. Round 8 did not replay that matrix because temporal physical completeness was unproved.

The 2023 center-oracle correction and historical references remain preserved. 1986 solar-radius convention/classification, invalid polar observer references, independent numerical lunar obscuration and physical temporal envelope proof remain open. Synthetic temporal/topological tests do not certify real events. accuracy_validated=False, certified_accuracy_interval=None. 1972-2100 is an experimental computational domain, not certified precision; explicit TT/UT1 and refusal outside supported inputs remain required.
